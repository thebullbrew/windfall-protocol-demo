◆ Rules of the Protocol

Print them. Tape them to the fridge. They've saved more money than any stock tip ever will.

  1. Ninety days of boring beats nine days of thrilling — every single time.
  2. The first job of new money is to stop being new. Park it somewhere dull.
  3. Taxes get estimated early and confirmed once, in writing. The IRS does not accept vibes.
  4. Double-digit APR is a fire. You don't invest around a fire — you put it out.
  5. Fun money is capped at 5%. Fun is a line item, not a leak.
  6. Nobody needs to know. The fastest way to halve a windfall is to announce it.
  7. A freed payment is a soldier reassigned, never a soldier discharged. It rolls to the next fight.
