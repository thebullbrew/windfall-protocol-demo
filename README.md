# The Windfall Protocol — Demo

**This is the free, locked demo of The Windfall Protocol — a 90-day command system for a lump sum: inheritance, bonus, settlement, or business sale.**

The demo runs one fixed sample scenario — a **$250,000 inheritance**, four debts, a $3,850 monthly essentials number — with every input locked, so you can see exactly what the Protocol computes: the tax set-aside, the avalanche kill order, the interest saved versus minimums alone, the emergency-fund top-up, and the Day 61–90 allocation with its guardrails (fun money capped at 5%, a warning when illiquid bets run hot). The 90-day checklist is view-only here, with a few boxes pre-checked so you can see how progress reads.

## The full version

The full Windfall Protocol — sold as a one-time purchase at [thebullbrew.com](https://www.thebullbrew.com) — runs **your** windfall: your amount and source, your debts in kill order, your interest saved to the dollar, allocation sliders that answer back, and a live 90-day checklist that saves your progress on your own device. No subscription. No account. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

The four phases, in order, no skipping:

1. **Days 1–7 · Freeze** — park the money, tell no one, no purchase over your computed cap.
2. **Days 8–30 · Tax & Shield** — set-aside moved aside and confirmed with a CPA; will, beneficiaries, and insurance put in order.
3. **Days 31–60 · Debt Triage** — the lump lands in avalanche order, highest APR first; freed minimums roll downhill.
4. **Days 61–90 · Allocate** — emergency fund topped first, then goal buckets under the guardrails.

## How to run

Open `docs/index.html` in any modern browser, or visit the live demo. Works from `file://`, installs as a PWA, runs offline once loaded.

*Not financial, tax, or legal advice. Tax set-aside percentages are planning estimates; confirm your actual obligation with a CPA or tax attorney.*
